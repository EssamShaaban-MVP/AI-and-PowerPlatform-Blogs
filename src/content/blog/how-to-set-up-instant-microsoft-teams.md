## Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.
Learn in this blog how you can easily automate it in just 1 minute with AI Builder and Power Automate! No advanced technical knowledge required.

- Go to the [Power Automate](https://flow.microsoft.com/) page. Click on the **“Templates”** tab located on the left-hand side of the page.
- In the search bar, type in **“Notify me on Teams when I receive an email with negative sentiment”**.
- The template should appear in the search results. Click on it to open the template.

![Figure 1: Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.](/blog-images/how-to-set-up-instant-microsoft-teams/01.webp)

- You will be redirected to the template’s details page.
- You will be prompted to sign in to each connector using your Microsoft account if you haven’t already done so.
- Click on the **“Continue”** button to proceed.

![Figure 2: Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.](/blog-images/how-to-set-up-instant-microsoft-teams/02.webp)

- You will be navigated to the flow designer page, and you will notice that the template uses AI Builder to analyze sentiment in Outlook emails.

![Figure 3: Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.](/blog-images/how-to-set-up-instant-microsoft-teams/03.webp)

- Ensure that all actions are authenticated and configured with the correct parameters.
- Click on the **“Save”** button to save your flow.
- Finally, initiate the flow by clicking on the "Run" button and test its functionality by sending a negative email to the designated email address.

![Figure 4: Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.](/blog-images/how-to-set-up-instant-microsoft-teams/04.webp)

- Upon receiving the email, you will receive a Teams notification with the following message.

![Figure 5: Do you regularly receive a ton of emails and want to be notified immediately over Teams when you receive an email with negative sentiment.](/blog-images/how-to-set-up-instant-microsoft-teams/05.webp)

That’s it! You have successfully set up the “Notify me on Teams when I receive an email with negative sentiment” template. Now, whenever you receive a negative email, you will receive a new message in Teams.
